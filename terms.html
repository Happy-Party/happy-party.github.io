2.2 3D-Printed Novelty Items: Several of our themed bags include custom 3D-printed items (including, but not limited to, 3D dinosaurs, dragons, skates, clown figures, keyrings, and busts). These items are novelty keepsakes crafted from PLA (polylactic acid) or similar filaments. They are not indestructible and may break into smaller, sharp, or ingestible pieces if chewed, dropped, stepped on, or subjected to rough play. They must never be placed in the mouth.
